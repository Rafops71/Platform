# MyPlatform — trailer (45-60 s), English and Spanish

Opens every film; the walkthrough follows. Animated (OpenMontage, Remotion),
dark blue #1d4a6d and white, the MyPlatform wordmark. No real names, no
real screens: icons and motion only.

| # | Seconds | Picture | English | Español |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-5 | Dark blue. "MyPlatform" fades in, a thin line draws under it. | A private market for physical commodities. By invitation only. | Un mercado privado de materias primas físicas. Solo por invitación. |
| 2 | 5-15 | Two trader icons far apart; a question mark between them; a handshake that cracks. | In commodity trading, you rarely know who is real, and showing your hand too early can cost you the deal. | En el comercio de materias primas, rara vez se sabe quién es real, y mostrar sus cartas demasiado pronto puede costarle la operación. |
| 3 | 15-30 | Two members left and right, faces blurred; an Operator in the middle; messages travel only through the middle; at the end both faces sharpen as a handshake forms. | On MyPlatform, members never see each other. Every message goes through an Operator, who introduces both sides only when both agree. | En MyPlatform, los miembros nunca se ven entre sí. Cada mensaje pasa por un Operador, que presenta a ambas partes solo cuando las dos están de acuerdo. |
| 4 | 30-45 | Three cards pop in one by one: a lock, a tick, a handshake. | Discretion: your name stays private until you choose.
