# AGREED 23 Nov 2026: documents wording on listings (owner's decision, "Yes" after seeing the full text)

Replaces the interim "Documents seen by poster". Sources: Grok and ChatGPT were asked the same box; they agree on the answers
(owner pasted both, 23 Nov). The supervisor's own web searches (five) found NO page using "documents sighted", "advised as
available" or "documents held" in an offer, so this is NOT verified trade standard; ChatGPT itself says "sighted" is real business
wording but not a standardised commodity-offer status. The owner decided knowing this.

## English (final, owner approved)
Member-facing statement lines (Browse, and the Operator's Read panel shows the same words; the Operator console stays English):
- "Poster:" becomes "Listing member:".
- "Stated by the poster on {date}" becomes "Stated by the listing member on {date}".
- Broker or mandated intermediary: label "Supporting documentation status". Answers: "Documents sighted" / "Documents not sighted" / "Documents not sighted; availability advised by source".
- Principal (producer or trader): label "Supporting documentation". Answers: "Documentation held and available" / "Documentation not currently held".
- The fixed line "Documents: after the introduction, subject to agreement between the parties." becomes "Document exchange: after the introduction, subject to agreement between the parties."

Posting form, SELL offers only (a Buy request is not asked, as today):
- Broker / mandated intermediary: "Have you sighted supporting documentation for this offer?" with the three broker answers above.
- Principal: "Do you hold the supporting documentation for this offer?" with the two principal answers above.

Rules: existing answers are NOT deleted (additive database change only); display follows the new rule. Neither field may imply the
platform, the Operator or the member has verified the authenticity or sufficiency of any document.
