# Member film EN, owner-approved 04-11 (blocks 1-4, version A each)
1 Welcome to MyPlatform. Sign in with the invitation you received. The first time, you sign the Terms once. To post an opportunity, open New Listing. Choose buy or sell, then enter the commodity, quantity, origin and Incoterm. State your role and declare the documents you have available. Submit, and your listing goes live.
2 In My Listings, you see and manage your own opportunities. In Browse, you see what other members are offering and seeking. You see the opportunity, never the member behind it. Interested? Press Contact and write your message.
3 Every message goes to MyPlatform's Operator, who relays it to the other side. Neither side learns who the other is. Replies arrive in your Mailbox. Documents are never shared through MyPlatform: they pass only between the parties, after the introduction.
4 A dot on Notifications tells you something new has arrived. In Profile, you keep your details and choose English or Spanish; Help answers common questions. When both sides agree on the essential terms and wish to proceed: introductions are made, and direct access between the parties is granted only after commission agreement has been executed. Each member then proceeds on their own judgement and due diligence. MyPlatform: where opportunity meets discretion.
