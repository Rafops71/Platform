# Operator film EN, owner-approved 04-11 (blocks 1-4, version A each)
1 This is the Operator console. Overview shows today's workload at a glance. In Approvals, you review each new registration and approve or decline it. MyPlatform is by invitation only: in Invitations, you create and send invitation links. In Users, you see every member and can suspend an account when needed.
2 All Listings shows every opportunity on the platform, with filters. In Commodities, you keep the list members choose from. The Mailbox is the heart of your work. Every member message arrives here first. Read it, then forward it to the other side, reply yourself, or set it aside. Before forwarding, remove anything that reveals identity: names, companies, emails, phone numbers or links. The platform highlights them for you.
3 Matches suggests buy and sell opportunities that fit together, so you can bring the right parties into conversation. In Deals, a file opens by itself the first time you forward a message about a listing. Keep its stage up to date. Once both sides agree on the essential terms, the commission agreement is executed first; only then are introductions made and direct access granted.
4 Scorecard counts what was recorded in deal files for each member: facts, not a verdict. Analytics shows how the platform is used: listings, messages and deals over time. The Activity Log records who did what, and when. And Help answers common questions. Discretion is the product: every message you handle protects it.
