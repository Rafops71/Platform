# DRAFT - Terms 3.6 - NOT APPLIED, NOT SIGNED BY ANYONE

Members sign this text, so the wording is the OWNER's choice. For each new or
changed clause below, two short options are given in English. Nothing here is
built, migrated or published. No pull request is opened for it.

Checked against the current Terms (js/i18n.js, terms.s3.body, terms.s5.body,
terms.s7.body, English, 3.5).

## A. No account sharing (new sentence in section 3, after the credentials sentence)

Current text (section 3) ends: "You are responsible for keeping your credentials
secure and for all activity carried out under your account."

Overlap: that sentence already makes the member responsible for activity on the
account. The new sentence must not repeat it word for word; it adds the rule
that the account is not to be shared.

- Option 1: "You must not let anyone else use your account or share your
  login details."
- Option 2: "Your account is for you alone. Do not give your login details to
  anyone, and do not allow anyone else to act through it."

## B. Reinforced members' responsibilities (new sentence in section 5, after "You are solely responsible for your own due diligence")

Current text (section 5) already says the member is solely responsible for due
diligence, and section 7 already says the statements a member makes when they
publish a listing are "statements made by you under these Terms". The new
sentence must extend those, not contradict them.

- Option 1: "Every statement you make on the Platform, including about goods,
  documents and your capacity, is yours, and you warrant that it is accurate
  and that you have the authority you claim."
- Option 2: "You are responsible for the accuracy of what you state on the
  Platform, and you warrant that you hold any authority you claim to hold, before
  any commitment is made."
