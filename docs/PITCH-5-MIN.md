MyPlatform: a five-minute talk (spoken)

1. What it is

MyPlatform is a private place where people who buy and sell physical commodities find each other. You can look for the right buyer or seller without the whole market seeing your business. It solves one problem: finding the right counterparty, and keeping control of who knows what. The design is simple: a private room, one trusted go-between, and a record of every deal.

2. Who it is for

It is for brokers, intermediaries and principals in physical commodity markets. It is invitation-only. Nobody can join by applying. The Operator invites you, and the Operator approves you before you can sign in. It is not a public marketplace. Nothing is published to the public. The sign-in page says so too: access is by invitation only.

3. What you see

You post an opportunity. You choose buy or sell, the commodity, the quantity, the origin and the Incoterm. You state your role. You say which documents you have. When you submit, it goes live. You can edit or withdraw your own listings at any time, and an old listing prompts you to renew it in one action. In Browse, you see what other members offer and seek. You see the opportunity and the poster's role. You never see the name or the company behind it. Your own messages and replies sit in your own mailbox, and nobody else's.

4. How a contact works

Step one: you see a listing you like. Step two: you press Contact and write a message. Step three: the message goes to the Operator, not to the other side. Step four: the Operator reads it, then forwards it, replies, or sets it aside. Step five: when the Operator forwards it, the other side gets your message, and neither side learns who the other is. The first time a message about a listing is forwarded, a deal file opens by itself, one for each pair of parties. Step six: when both sides agree the essential terms, the commission agreement is signed first. Only then are introductions made and direct access given. Nothing is introduced automatically. The Operator must act.

5. What the Operator does

The Operator relays and checks every message. Before forwarding, the Operator removes names, companies, emails, phone numbers and links. The platform highlights them in red, and it will not forward a message that still carries one. The Operator does not request, receive, review or hold any document. Documents pass only between the two parties, after the introduction. The original of any edited message stays in a private log that only the Operator can see.

6. The Terms, in plain words

The platform is provided free of charge to members. It is private and invitation-only. You must not try to find out who another member is, and you must not go around the Operator. The commission rule lasts 24 months after an introduction, even if the deal closes outside the platform. You are responsible for your own checks and your own decisions. Nothing on the platform is advice. You keep your login to yourself. What you state on the platform must be accurate, and you must hold any authority you claim. If the Terms change in a material way, you accept the new version before you carry on.

7. Why this is better than doing it alone

You do not have to reveal who you are to find a buyer or a seller. A brokered channel checks what passes through it. Every contact goes through one place, so nothing gets lost. You decide what to send, and nothing moves without a choice you have made. Deal files record what happened in each negotiation. Listings, messages, deals and Help are all in one place, in English and Spanish.

8. Close

MyPlatform: where opportunity meets discretion. Access is by invitation only. If you want to join, ask an Operator for an invitation link.
