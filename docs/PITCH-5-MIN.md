# MyPlatform: the spoken talk

Read it aloud as written. The lines in brackets are for you, not to be spoken.

---

Let me start with what it is. MyPlatform is a private place where people who buy and sell physical commodities find each other. Most of the trade in these goods still runs on phone calls and personal contacts, and that has two problems. You do not know who is really on the other side, and once you have made an introduction, you can lose control of it. MyPlatform is built to fix both. It lets you look for the right counterparty while the rest of the market cannot see who you are, and it keeps every introduction inside one controlled channel.

It is for brokers, intermediaries and principals in physical commodity markets, and it is invitation-only. Nobody can join by applying. The Operator invites you, and the Operator approves you before you can sign in. It is not a public marketplace, and nothing on it is published to the public. The sign-in page says the same thing.

Let me show you what you see once you are in. You post an opportunity: whether you are buying or selling, the commodity, the quantity, the origin and the Incoterm. You say what role you are in the deal, and you say which documents you have available. The listing goes live when you submit it. You can edit or withdraw your own listings at any time. An old listing prompts you to renew it, in one action.

When you browse, you see what other members offer and what they are looking for. You see the opportunity and the poster's role. You do not see the name or the company behind it. That is the point of the platform. Your own messages and replies sit in your own mailbox, and nobody else's.

Now the important part: how a contact works. Suppose you see a listing you want to pursue. You press Contact and write your message. That message does not go to the other side. It goes to the Operator. The Operator reads it and decides what happens next: forward it, reply, or set it aside. When the Operator forwards it, the other side receives your message, and neither side learns who the other is. The first time a message about a listing is forwarded, a deal file opens by itself, one for each pair of parties, so the negotiation is recorded from the start.

Then comes the step that matters most. When both sides agree the essential terms, the commission agreement is signed first. Only after that are introductions made and direct access given. Nothing is introduced automatically. The Operator has to act, and the Operator will not make the introduction until the commission agreement has been executed.

That brings me to the Operator's role, which is the heart of the platform. The Operator relays and checks every message. Before anything is forwarded, the Operator removes names, companies, email addresses, phone numbers and links, because those are the things that let people go around the platform. The platform highlights them in red, and it will not forward a message that still carries one. The Operator does not request, receive, review or hold any document. Documents pass only between the two parties, and only after the introduction. If the Operator edits a message before forwarding it, the original is kept in a private log that only the Operator can see.

Let me explain the Terms in plain words, because they are what you agree to. The platform is provided free of charge to members. You must not try to find out who another member is, and you must not go around the Operator to deal with someone you were introduced to. The commission rule runs for twenty-four months after an introduction, even if the deal finishes outside the platform. That is a long tail, and you should read it closely. You are responsible for your own checks and your own decisions. Nothing on the platform is advice, and the Operator does not verify what members tell each other. You keep your login to yourself, and what you state on the platform must be accurate. You must also hold any authority you claim to have. If the Terms change in a material way, you must accept the new version before you carry on.

So why is this better than doing it alone? Because you do not have to reveal who you are to find a buyer or a seller. Every contact goes through one place, so nothing is lost between the first message and the deal. You decide what to send, and nothing moves without a choice you have made. And the record of each negotiation is kept, so you can see how a deal developed. Listings, messages, deals and help are all in one place, in English and Spanish.

To finish: MyPlatform is where opportunity meets discretion. Access is by invitation only. If you want to join, ask the Operator for an invitation link.
