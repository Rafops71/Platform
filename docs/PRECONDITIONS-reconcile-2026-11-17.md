## Questions that block a one-attempt build (all in the mockup)
1. After the member answers: the Operator forwards or ignores by hand. 2. The member answers with the normal Reply. 3. English only, or a Spanish text.
4. Which earlier statement is quoted (the sender's own listing, which tick). 5. The date: last confirmation of the whole listing, or the real date of each sentence.
6. The draft labels (box heading, the "on hold" status, when the button shows).

## What I could NOT check from here
The member's Mailbox screen as rendered for an `on_hold` message (nothing exists yet to render). Whether the owner wants the button when the sender has no earlier statement (mockup recommends: not offered).
